About

The short version of a longer story.

I spent my early twenties in service bays, not spreadsheets — running OBD-II scans, tracing electrical faults with a multimeter, and translating "check engine" into a plain-English explanation a customer could act on. That job taught me something a classroom can't: how to isolate one true cause inside a system full of noisy, conflicting signals.

While I worked, I was also finishing a B.S. in Economics with a Mathematics minor at the University of Houston, building the other half of the toolkit — regression analysis, statistics, and machine learning — that turns a hunch into a defensible answer. Data analysis is the place those two halves meet: pull the numbers apart, find the pattern underneath, and explain it so clearly that the next decision makes itself.

I'm most energized by problems with a customer on the other end of the answer — forecasting, churn, operations — anywhere a dataset is standing in for a real person trying to make a real decision.

FEATURED PROJECT · Trading & Market Analytics

Permian–Waha Basis Dashboard

Waha (Permian Basin) natural gas regularly trades at a discount to Henry Hub, the national benchmark — but no clean API exists for it anywhere. I built a pipeline that regex-parses the price directly out of narrative EIA reports, flags statistically unusual weeks with a rolling anomaly score, and tests storage and rig-count data as leading indicators — reporting the null result honestly alongside the real one.

FEATURED PROJECT · Pricing Analytics

Solar PPA Pricing & Discounting Analysis

A benchmark comparison isn't just a chart — it's only as good as understanding what the benchmark actually measures. Built a pipeline over LBNL's 1,775-project solar PPA database, caught a real methodology mistake before it shipped (a +$194/MWh "finding" that was really just an uncorrected vintage mismatch), and traced the deeper pattern to a mismatch between asking-price and realized-price data — confirmed directly against the benchmark provider's own published methodology.
